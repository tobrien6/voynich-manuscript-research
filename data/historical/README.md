# Historical interlinear source

`text16e6.zip` is an **unmodified publisher download**, kept separately from the
modern CC0 exports. Exact URLs, credits, byte lengths, and archive/member hashes
are in [interlinear-16e6.json](interlinear-16e6.json). The original member retains
the source's extensive comments and contributor credits.

The [publisher's Permissions section](https://www.ic.unicamp.br/~stolfi/EXPORT/00-EXPORT/98-12-28-interln16e6/)
permits reuse with due credit and without imposing additional conditions or
copyrights. Those source terms govern this archive; the repository's code
license does not relicense it. Credit Jorge Stolfi, Gabriel Landini, Jim Reeds,
Takeshi Takahashi, and the other transcribers identified in the member when using
their material. This statement concerns the transcription archive, not rights
to manuscript images.

This recovered release is a candidate input for historical-method replication.
Its equality to the bytes downloaded or processed by the 2013 authors remains
unproven. See the [native intake audit](../../research/historical/README.md).
