# Dee comparison references: distinct catalog targets

The older Yale catalog names Ashmole **487**, while the mediated quotation
attributed to Roberts and Watson names **847**. The accepted
[provenance audit](DEE_EVIDENCE.md#an-unresolved-specimen-reference) retains both.
This follow-up identifies the corresponding catalog descriptions; it does not
resolve the original quotation or reproduce its handwriting comparison.

## Primary catalog observations

William Henry Black’s **1845** Bodleian catalog is identified by the
[Library’s Quarto guide](https://libguides.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/modern-sc/quarto).
The inspected [public scan](https://archive.org/details/descriptiveanaly00bodl)
was contributed by Boston Public Library. Its title page gives Black, Oxford
University Press and 1845. The [ledger](dee-catalog-references.json) pins the
102,551,908-byte PDF and separates its file-page numbers from printed columns.
The total 964 file pages describe this scan, not the length of the 1845 text.

| Catalog target | Printed locator / PDF page, one-based | Description reported by Black |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ashmole 487 | Column 350 / page 181, grouped heading `No. 487, 488.` | First of two quarto ephemeris volumes: Stadius 1554–1600, printed 1570, with marginal diary notes attributed to Dee, January 1577–December 1600. |
| Ashmole 847 | Column 608 / page 310, `No. 847.` | A 134-leaf paper manuscript attributed to Robert Glover, containing Welsh vocabulary and historical material. Its reported gift note names Dee. |
| Ashmole 1790 | Columns 1496–1497 / pages 754–755, `No. 1790.` | Composite papers. Part I describes a Latin preface and narrative as written and corrected by its author Dee; Part III describes Ashmole’s observations and collections. |

These are catalog statements, not newly authenticated scribal attributions.
In particular, a body written by Glover does not identify the hand that added
folio numerals. A composite collection’s title does not assign every part to
one writer. The actual comparison leaf `1790, f. 9v` was not viewed.

## Separate guide check

The [microfilm guide](https://webdoc.sub.gwdg.de/zdmdm/mifoguide/matthew/RENAISSANCE_MAN_p1.pdf)
places 487 on reel 3 and 847 on reel 5. Its printed pages 54–55, 56–57 and
100–101 are PDF pages 25, 26 and 48. The retrieved guide has 95 file pages;
no publication year is assigned here. These listings reinforce the distinction
between reference targets. They are not the filmed manuscripts or a substitute
for inspecting Roberts and Watson’s original reported p. 172. Material repeated
between a guide and catalog is not independent manuscript evidence.

## Result and remaining test

**487 and 847 identify distinct catalog targets.** Both belong to a documentary
trail concerning Dee, so finding either number in a catalog does not establish
which comparison was intended, prove a typographical error, or support silent
correction. The existing ledger’s reference spellings remain unchanged.

Next inspect the original 1990 entry, authenticate the exact comparison leaves,
and register a numeral-selection rule and alternative contemporary hands before
comparing images. This note does not adjudicate Voynich foliation, ownership,
sale, cipher authorship, language or translation. No folio alias or corpus input
is changed.

## Verification and access

Retrieved and inspected 2026-10-12. OCR and PDF text supplied search leads;
complete rendered pages supplied the visual checks. An exact search for
`No. 487.` missed the grouped `No. 487, 488.` heading. Search failure alone would
have been insufficient evidence of absence.

Oxford’s archive record returned HTTP 403 and its older linked databank hostname
did not resolve. The independently accessible Archive scan matched its public
item checksum; the canonical redirect timed out, so the item’s advertised
secondary mirror was used. No access gate, authentication or institutional
contact was used. PDF identities, precise locators and inspection limits are in
the ledger; source files, extracted text and renders remain local.
