# Image-pair pilot for the accepted y-removal lookup diagnostic

## Question and exposure

For two fixed diagnostic cases in which removing leading literal EVA y gains
a development-vocabulary match, can the target and a fixed development occurrence
be located in their linked manuscript images, and what visible correspondence
or disagreement can be described? This tests the representation-to-image link,
not a layout-marker mechanism, glyph inventory, language, or translation.

The accepted parent is y-layout-prefix v1, corrected version
`1c6abb48776a6a59b527f502b50289edf9709191`, fully merged through PR #75.
Its full-corpus input, lookup outcomes, and related image reviews were already
exposed. The q/o and frequency/length controls in PR #109 are also known.
Some selected whole pages may have been viewed in earlier project inventories.
This is nonblind AI inspection on previously used candidate-development material,
not independent human adjudication, an unseen sample, or a candidate holdout.

## Selection fixed before pixels

1. Reconstruct the unchanged parent partition, all eight source/uncertainty
   treatments, common 3,002 eligible locations, development-only internal
   vocabularies, and original scientific report from byte-pinned inputs.
   Preserve parent exclusions and original slots. Select targets only from the
   parent's 15 all-treatment scorable diagnostic units.
2. A target is a common eligible locus with the same retained ASCII first-slot
   form in ZL3b/IT2a × split/join × exclude/first. It begins in lower-case y,
   has at least two letters, and removing exactly one y gives gain +1 in every
   treatment: the original is absent and the remainder present in each unchanged
   development vocabulary. Do not alter vocabulary construction for this pilot.
3. A reference is an occurrence of that exact remainder at an original non-first
   slot in the parent development locations. Its locus, original zero-based slot,
   and literal retained ASCII form must agree across all eight treatments. A
   representation match is not a glyph or word identity.
4. Targets and reference loci must have no `<->` or `<~>` drawing interruption
   outside free comments in either edition. Each page needs exactly one Yale
   canvas with an exact single-side label and no partial/multiple-side flag.
   No panel, legacy terminal-folio, or physical-row alias is inferred.
5. Sort targets by numeric folio, recto before verso, then numeric locus. For
   each target, sort qualifying references by that same order, then original
   zero-based slot. Choose its earliest reference. Select the earliest target
   from each of the first **two distinct parent dependency units**. Reference
   units may repeat across pairs; retain that dependence explicitly. Preserve
   all candidate targets/reference identifiers, exclusions, and unfilled slots.
6. Commit and publish this plan before selection and any new image inspection.
   Commit the resulting selector and complete selection report before inspecting
   selected image pixels. Do not change the chosen pair for readability, a
   favorable match, or an unavailable crop. No additional pair is substituted.

## Inspection and records

Inspect both whole images for each pair before inspecting original-pixel row
and token-region derivatives. Regions may be chosen only after whole-image
orientation, and their selection is a manual judgment rather than a frozen
algorithm. Record native image/service metadata, requested URLs, coordinates,
dimensions, byte hashes, access date, and any unavailable request. Images stay
local; publish links and original observations, plus limited licensed native
source/context extracts with edition/file-line/slot identities.

Use the source publisher's Basic Eva table only as a shape reference. Compare
the proposed target row with its source neighbors and layout, and similarly
locate the development row and internal group. Distinguish visible strokes and
spacing, provisional locus/token alignment, and comparison interpretation.
Record plausible alternative placements and native uncertainty. If a row or
token cannot be supported, mark that pair unresolved and stop its finer comparison.
Do not infer a physical token index by counting encoded slots alone.

Compare whether the target's initial cluster and remaining cluster can be
described separately, and whether the remainder resembles the reference along
explicit visible features. Different forms, uncertain boundaries, or partial
correspondence must be recorded. A positive resemblance is provisional, does
not equate encoded letters with glyphs, and cannot determine morphology versus
formatting or other mechanisms. No numerical similarity score or accuracy claim
is assigned. No source correction, verified alignment, or statistical rerun is
authorized by this image pilot.

## Validation and next evidence

Synthetic tests must cover eight-treatment intersection, absent-original/
present-remainder lookup gains, original slots, development/evaluation roles,
source/spacing disagreement, drawing exclusion outside comments, unique canvas
labels, numeric/slot ordering, dependency-unit sampling, empty selections, and
complete accounting. Selector replay must preserve all inputs and selected cases.
Output guards must preserve inputs and producer/helper files, including linked
aliases. The image record must bind to the frozen selection and plan and retain
both attempts, explicit unverified/correction flags, region bounds and failures.

Independent human review of the same regions and explicit versioned glyph/token
decisions remain necessary before treating either pair as ground truth. This
pilot supplies checkable evidence at the current lookup/image gap and retains
the original diagnostic and all its limits.
