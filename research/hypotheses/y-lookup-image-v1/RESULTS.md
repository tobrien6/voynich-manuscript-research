# Two y-removal lookup gains traced to provisional image pairs

Both fixed pairs can be provisionally located using the selected native contexts
and their neighboring rows. Each target has an initial curled form before a body
with broad visible similarities to its selected internal reference. This adds
checkable image evidence to two lookup cases; neither pair establishes glyph
identity, an added layout marker, a word, a language, or a translation.

## Selection and identities

The original parent partition, source selection, 3,002 common eligible loci,
development-only dictionaries, and complete scientific results replay unchanged.
Of 563 eligible diagnostic loci, 16 qualify as targets and 547 have explicit
exclusion reasons. Targets must belong to the parent's 15 common scoring units,
share the exact first form across all eight treatments, and gain a match after
removing exactly one y in every treatment. The original must be absent from
every unchanged dictionary; its remainder must be present.

An internal reference must preserve the same locus, original slot, and exact
remainder in all eight treatments, belong to development material, have no
inline drawing interruption in either edition, and have a unique exact-side
Yale canvas. The global occurrence intersection contains 10,790 development
occurrences; each queried remainder's eligible and excluded stable occurrences
remain in the report. Source/policy-dependent occurrences cannot qualify.
The earliest qualifying reference is fixed for each target. Targets are ordered
by numeric folio/side/locus, selecting the first two distinct dependency units;
no pair is replaced after seeing pixels.

## Inspection method

Inspected 12 October 2026, America/New_York. View each whole-page overview
(`full/!1600,1600`) before its row-context and group windows. Overview heights
are 1,600 pixels; native dimensions remain 2,649 × 3,743 for f17r/f18r,
2,849 × 3,769 for f19v, and 2,853 × 3,769 for f35v. Each linked window below
requests native pixels using `x,y,width,height`; no rescaling or pixel editing
was used for those windows. Service metadata and JPEG/GIF dimensions and byte
hashes identify the inspected resources in [image-review.json](image-review.json).

The publisher's [Basic Eva table](https://voynich.nu/img/extra/eva01.gif), linked
from its [transliteration description](https://voynich.nu/transcr.html), supplies
shape references. These are notation labels, not phonetic readings. Its first
download returned HTTP 406; a later ordinary browser-style request succeeded.
That failed request and the successful bytes are both recorded. Manual windows
were chosen after page orientation and remain judgments requiring review.
Only URLs, fingerprints, native extracts and original observations are published;
the downloaded pixels stay local.

## f17r.2 and f18r.6: broad low-form correspondence with a pale beginning

The proposed target is the second row of f17r's top three-row block. The elevated
opening above it and the shorter low-starting row below it are compatible with
the preserved f17r.1/f17r.3 contexts. The target's native rows are ZL line 700
and IT line 411. Its following group differs as ZL `choky` versus IT `shoky`;
this difference is not resolved by the present comparison.

Inspect the [f17r row context](https://collections.library.yale.edu/iiif/2/1006106/300,440,2040,310/full/0/default.jpg)
and [initial group](https://collections.library.yale.edu/iiif/2/1006106/320,560,520,170/full/0/default.jpg).
A pale looped beginning with a descending/curved stroke is followed closely by
a compact double-lobed shape, low angled strokes, and an upturned terminal
curve. Green wash and low contrast limit fine separation of the beginning.

The proposed reference is the next paragraph's second row on f18r, following
the tall t-starting f18r.5 and preceding the bud-interrupted f18r.7. Its selected
group follows a low curled group and a tall looped group; relative order and
the surrounding sequence support the f18r.6 association. Native reference rows
are ZL line 760 and IT line 452.

Inspect the [f18r row context](https://collections.library.yale.edu/iiif/2/1006108/230,680,1600,350/full/0/default.jpg)
and [internal group](https://collections.library.yale.edu/iiif/2/1006108/610,775,330,155/full/0/default.jpg).
The darker group has a double-lobed initial, low angled strokes, and a prominent
curved/upturned ending. Those broad features are compatible with the sequence
after the target's initial curled form. A y-like beginning is plausible against
the notation table, but its identity and separation are unresolved. No distinct
word-space boundary after it is established. Different contrast and handwriting
prevent treating this as exact glyph recovery.

## f19v.4 and f35v.15: tall-loop and low-form correspondence

The proposed target is the upper paragraph's fourth row on f19v, between the
longer f19v.3 and q-starting f19v.5. The initial group is followed by another
tall-looped cluster and two repeated terminal groups, compatible with the shared
source sequence. The native rows are ZL line 818 and IT line 490. Relative row
order and the neighboring first/terminal patterns support this placement over
the immediately adjacent rows; no exhaustive alignment search is claimed.

Inspect the [f19v row context](https://collections.library.yale.edu/iiif/2/1006111/870,580,1250,290/full/0/default.jpg)
and [initial group](https://collections.library.yale.edu/iiif/2/1006111/900,670,420,170/full/0/default.jpg).
A curled descending beginning precedes paired tall stems with looped tops,
followed by a low horizontal and curved form. Dark speckling affects later
groups on the row.

The proposed f35v reference lies between the tall-starting f35v.14 and the low
double-lobed beginning of f35v.16. Its final group follows three broader groups
and ends near the leaf, compatible with `ykol.cheol.okchy.tch`. The native
reference rows are ZL line 1497 and IT line 912. Its row-end position and low
following form distinguish it from the preceding tall cluster.

Inspect the [f35v row context](https://collections.library.yale.edu/iiif/2/1006143/850,1530,1000,430/full/0/default.jpg)
and [final group](https://collections.library.yale.edu/iiif/2/1006143/1420,1630,330,180/full/0/default.jpg).
It has paired tall stems and looped tops followed by a low horizontal and curved
form. One stem/top is more open and rectangular than in the target. The body
after the target initial broadly resembles this reference, while the target has
an additional preceding curled shape. This is compatible with the literal y
comparison, but the y/tch boundary, glyph identities and prefix function are not
adjudicated.

## Reproduce and limitations

Standard-library Python 3.10+, with the frozen Git history available:

```sh
python3 -m unittest tests.test_y_lookup_images tests.test_y_lookup_review tests.test_y_layout_prefix tests.test_initial_prefix_images
python3 scripts/select_y_lookup_images.py --check research/hypotheses/y-lookup-image-v1/selection.json
python3 scripts/verify_y_lookup_review.py
python3 scripts/verify_y_lookup_review.py --local-directory /path/to/recorded/files
```

The optional directory binds every recorded image/service filename to its exact
bytes and dimensions; these files are not committed. The verifier checks frozen
plan/selection blobs, both selected attempts, unverified flags, observations,
resource completeness, source URL identities and native bounds. Automated checks
cannot assess whether a row association or visible description is correct.
All 45 relevant tests pass, including ten new selection tests, seven record
tests, and the accepted parent/initial-prefix selector tests. The complete
selection and every local image/service fingerprint replay.

These two deliberately selected positive lookup cases are not an accuracy sample
or independent confirmation of a mechanism. Related editions can agree while
remaining wrong; resemblance can reflect handwriting, morphology, formatting,
segmentation, or transcription. Repeated source/treatment agreement and separate
page-family labels do not create independent glyph evidence. The next step is
independent human review of these exact regions and alternative placements,
followed by an explicit versioned glyph/token decision before any source change
or renewed statistical interpretation. The original diagnostic and its
prior-exposure limits remain intact.

The [local pair reviewer](viewer/README.md) displays these same accepted native
contexts and recorded whole/row/group windows, with image-byte checks and
separately collapsible original AI observations. It adds an inspection interface
without changing either selected pair, its images, the observations or status.
