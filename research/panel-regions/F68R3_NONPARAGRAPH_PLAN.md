# f68r3 non-paragraph review plan

Freeze this packet before this contribution's first manuscript-pixel inspection.
The accepted 68r layout/inventory images, page conventions and source counts
have already been exposed in the project. Both complete native f68r3 record
sets and ZL's descriptive comments were read before writing this plan. This
is a non-blind AI-assisted development review, not independent glyph validation.

## Fixed source scope and anchor attempts

- Keep all 22 present native f68r3 locations, numbered 1–22, in both pinned
  ZL3b and IT2a. Require their page headers, full ordered key sets, native
  kinds/locators and source identities. Preserve text, extended glyph notation,
  uncertainty, free comments, spaces and source-file line references.
- Retain both Cc records (1, 22), all eight Ri records (2–9), all eleven Ls
  records (10, 11, 13–21), and the La record (12). Preserve the zero P0 count;
  neither 22 locations nor eleven Ls records implies that many separate words,
  physical lines, stars or independent manuscript observations.
- Attempt image association for exactly the first and last native Ri records,
  f68r3.2 and f68r3.9. The rule uses source order, not text agreement, length,
  readability or apparent glyph identity. Retain a failed/unlocated attempt.
  Every other record stays in the packet with glyph association unreviewed.
- ZL's source comment identifies a radial sequence beginning at 10:30 and refers
  to ThP ordering. Its two circular records contain 09:30 free comments. These
  are source annotations to check, not image coordinates or verified physical
  reading directions. Do not infer the remaining radial angles from sequence
  numbers, import an uninspected ThP diagram, or force clockwise ordering.

## Image and specification inspection

Use Yale canvas/image 1006196 on the shared 68r carrier, preserving its leaf/scan
dependency across all three native panel names. First inspect the official
2,400-pixel-bounded whole-carrier derivative. Then choose one native-coordinate
rectangle containing the complete right circular drawing and its surrounding
writing. Choose its extent from geometry, not reading agreement; record it as
an inspection window rather than a precise panel or glyph mask.

Allow at most two detail windows per anchor attempt after the overview, with
official IIIF rotation only when needed to view oriented writing. Retain all
request URLs, coordinates, rotations, response identities/dimensions and failed
requests. Do not inspect additional loci to replace a difficult anchor. Source
pixels remain local; publish links, metadata and original observations.

Consult IVTFF document issue 2.0.2, Tables 8/9, printed pages 21/22, for native
locator/type meanings. Its SHA-256 must match the accepted page-convention
record. The specification defines source categories; it does not certify that
a particular manuscript feature has been correctly transcribed or located.

## Report and preserve limits

Separate native source claims, directly visible shapes/layout and proposed
associations. Retain both full source readings and uncertainty for every
attempt. Use provisional, contradicted or unlocated statuses as warranted;
leave exact glyph alignment, word boundaries and independent review pending.
Do not edit a source, convert alphabets, infer meaning from an illustration,
choose a decipherment or produce a translation.

Record geometry and drawing interruptions without turning creases into equal
panel masks. Relative locator codes refer to preceding native numbers and do
not automatically group records into labels or semantic units. Preserve the
source absence/zero distinction and keep the original pilot-readiness gate
unchanged. This packet does not become a verified corpus join, untouched
evaluation material, or evidence that related editions are independent.
