certification of token boundaries. The longer preceding row supports the
proposed f76r.55 context without resolving its ZL/IT reading differences.

## Interpretation and further review

No inspected adjacent placement supplied a better broad match under these
specific cues. That does not rule out every possible association. The endpoint
placements rely heavily on source/page order; the median also uses the paired
terminal forms. Compatibility with related transcriptions is weaker evidence
than an independently adjudicated glyph sequence.

This is another AI-assisted check in the Codex environment. Model independence
is not established, and the first observer's interpretation was available.
All three attempts share one already exposed page, the same derivatives and
two related editions. They are not three independent manuscript samples,
an independent human validation, or untouched candidate-evaluation material.

Keep the original mapping status provisional, glyph adjudication unresolved,
and source corrections absent. A specialist should next check the complete
rows and neighboring alternatives, especially the initial readings and uncertain
spaces. Accepted token-level boxes and physical-line mappings need separate
review. No decoding, language identification, translation or statistical rerun
is established by this comparison. Images remain local; only Yale links,
fingerprints, references and original observations are published.
