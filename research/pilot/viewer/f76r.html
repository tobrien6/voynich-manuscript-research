Native notation and source conventions

The displayed strings are encoded transliterations, not established plaintext. Case, dots, commas, alternatives and tags are shown literally. In IVTFF, a period marks a certain word boundary, a comma an uncertain one, square brackets preserve reading alternatives, and question marks preserve unreadability. <%> and <$> are source paragraph annotations.
