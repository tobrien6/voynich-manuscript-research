## Question and limits

Can the discrepant paragraph-end marks be associated with an identifiable text
line, and what visible layout evidence bears on a boundary after that line?
Count and order generic P loci, keeping other P subtypes. Associate a source
locus with a physical line only where locators and the visible ordered layout
support a unique match. Numbering alone is insufficient where text is fragmented
or multiple blocks could be ordered differently; retain uncertainty explicitly.

Record direct observations about text-block position, line lengths, spacing,
and illustration interruptions separately from source annotations and inferred
boundaries. Do not transcribe glyphs, assign meaning, correct source files, or
claim independent human review. These are AI-assisted initial observations.

A boundary disposition is supported only when line association is unambiguous
and visible block separation supplies clear evidence; otherwise retain it as
unresolved. A short line by itself is insufficient. Neither matching annotations
nor one visual preference is a validated decipherment. This three-page sample
is purposive and cannot estimate manuscript-wide annotation accuracy.
