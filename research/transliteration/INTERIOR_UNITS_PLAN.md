# Interior-order unit sensitivity: exploratory plan v1

Publish this plan and the synthetic-tested implementation before applying
grouped count policies to the manuscript cohort or generating new controls.
This question follows the already inspected interior-order and half-position
results. The full corpus and the grouping policies are previously exposed.
It is a measurement sensitivity study, not a new evaluation set, registered
decipherment candidate, fitted production mechanism or translation.

## Question, source and fixed cohort

Does the interior-order departure persist under the project's three declared
EVA count policies, with both unrestricted and early/late-half controls, when
the original cohort and token-index permutations are held fixed?

Reuse every one of the original **2,165 common P0 loci**, four ZL3b/IT2a
split/join variants, original token boundaries, native case, conservative
whole-locus exclusions and minimum five-token rule. Recompute and verify the
complete original selection/exclusions, source hashes, ASCII vectors,
denominators, observations and expectations before generating controls.
Grouping introduces no exclusions, retokenization, new source or new split.
The first/last original tokens remain outside the interior statistic and
permutations. The crossing interior pair remains included.

Reuse the exact three lists and longest-match segmenter in
[the accepted unit protocol](../baselines/EVA_UNIT_PROTOCOL.md) and its
[settings](../baselines/eva-unit-protocol.json): ASCII letters, listed bench
sequences, and listed bench plus minim sequences. Unmatched letters retain
single-letter fallback; no lowercasing or cross-token match is allowed.
Concatenated segments must reconstruct each original token. Count the emitted
segments per interior token. Never add a grouping because of its result.

Landini's [EVA reference](https://voynich.nu/hist/gabriel/eva2_reference.pdf)
was downloaded and visually checked on pages 4 and 6–7 for this follow-up.
Its 170,416 bytes match accepted SHA-256
`2f9a9c7e81dedfbd934e49eeac47de07e7e0855eda46f063a0624958d3e524fc`.
It documents notation connectivity and alphabet correspondences, not an
adjudicated manuscript glyph inventory. Reuse the project's finite operational
policies rather than claiming a complete historical-alphabet conversion.
The PDF and its rendered pages stay outside the contribution.

## Paired controls and measures

Use the existing normalized interior association R, unrestricted centered C,
and exact half-centered C defined in the parent plans. The half split remains
`ceil(m/2)` in original interior token slots; grouping changes counts, not slots.
Reuse the exact rational half expectation. Constants contribute zero.

Use 999 trials 0–998 and seed 20261011. For each original source/spacing
variant, use its **original ASCII length-corpus digest** in the accepted full
and half permutation key recipes. Generate a permutation of original interior
token indices and apply it identically to all three count vectors. The mapped
policy digest is recorded but does not replace the ASCII draw key. This pairs
every control with the same token order and reproduces the original ASCII
draws. Half permutations use separate early/late block keys as before. Identical
numerical input/stream combinations may share calculation; audits and dependent
treatment records remain separate. IT split/join remain one dependent source
comparison.

Report all **24** source/spacing/unit/control combinations and every draw.
Each locus has equal weight. For each of the two grouped policies also retain
the observed change from ASCII and every paired grouped-minus-ASCII control
difference under each control scope: **16** complete paired contrasts.
Do not subtract independent arrays or present paired changes as physical
differences in a common glyph unit or a causal fraction explained.

Retain uncentered effects, exact expectations, centered effects, control means
and standard deviations, per-page descriptive means, count-vector digests,
interior/pair/locus denominators and original cohort hash. Preserve the existing
two-sided Monte Carlo convention with 1e-12 comparison tolerance and
`(1 + exceedances)/1000`, including the 0.001 resolution floor. Report all
directions and nonsmall tails; the earlier 0.01 convention is exploratory,
without a new family-level significance or confidence claim.

Audit only the selected **interior tokens**: emitted pattern counts, uppercase
single-letter fallback, occurrences of the accepted unlisted examples, total
letters/segments, and newly constant locus identities. Reuse the accepted exact
half-invariance classifier; distinguish exact invariant cases from the narrower
both-halves-constant count. Never remove these cases from a denominator.

## Verification and interpretation

Before manuscript execution, test longest-match/case/fallback behavior and
lossless source reconstruction, unchanged token boundaries and endpoints,
identity of paired index permutations, full/half multiset preservation,
exact centering on exhaustive small cases, constant-collapse retention,
paired differences and full-report reproduction, changed-pin/cohort rejection,
and input/output aliases before calculation and final write.

Replay the complete saved report afterward. Independently calculate observed
effects from exact rational deviations/expectations, and check selected paired
draws from explicit token-index permutations. Keep all unfavorable variants.

Persistence would constrain these count representations under the stated
controls; it would not establish direct neighbor causation, physical glyphs,
language or plaintext. Loss of a departure would expose representation
sensitivity rather than prove randomness. The finite maps omit connected forms;
partial matches and fallback remain visible. The conservative cohort, source
conventions, finer positional patterns, copying and prior exposure remain
limitations. Further candidate mechanisms need explicit representations,
appropriate controls and prospective tests under the decipherment protocol.
