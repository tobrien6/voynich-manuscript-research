# Historical B fixed-trial tail plan, version 1

Question: how often do additional controls from the accepted native-record bag
null produce B at least as large, or at least as far from its exact mean, as the
observed B on each of the four unchanged approximate H streams?

Publish this plan and synthetic-tested producer before calculating new source
trials. All source/parser/mean results, original forty controls, join census,
mean figure and the submitted variance outcomes are already exposed. This is
exploratory simulation on known data, not a fresh hypothesis, untouched evaluation
or evidence of independent replication. No significance cutoff is chosen.

## Immutable parent and scored population

Pin the complete accepted conditional mean/control report and its nineteen
source/method/plan/amendment dependencies. Require complete parent report replay,
all four record/flat corpus hashes, original observations, both A/B counts and
all boundary diagnostics. The new study depends on the accepted mean/control
study, not on the separately reviewed variance implementation.

B is the distinct complete representation-type count with one or more equal
adjacent pairs over all N−1 surviving flat-stream edges. Native-record joins,
normalized index-gap edges and retained asterisks remain as in the parent. No
new corpus filter, exclusion, tokenization, tail-based policy selection or
physical-line interpretation is introduced.

## Exactly one new trial block

Use the parent's declared independent native-record bag permutations, accepted
SHA-256 counter/Fisher–Yates primitive and unchanged `historical-native-record-v1`
frame. Required seed is 20261012. Generate exactly 1,000 new trials with IDs
10–1009 for each variant, disjoint from its ten existing IDs 0–9. Each trial
starts from the original retained tokens in every record. Preserve every bag,
retained length and provenance slot. Duplicated outcomes/statistics are retained;
no control deduplication or sampling without replacement is attempted.

Retain all new trial IDs, complete A/B/asterisk count diagnostics and SHA-256 of
the encoded flat token vector for each control. Keep the ten original control
rows separately and require they replay unchanged; they are not pooled into the
new tail counts. Use four fixed process workers for independent case computation,
then collect results in the declared ring/M/L matrix order. Worker completion
order affects neither keys nor outputs. Finish all 1,000 trials in every case;
never extend, shorten, restart or stop a trial block based on its outcomes.

## Both inclusive tail definitions and exact score arithmetic

For observed b and the parent's exact fixed mean mu, count:

```text
upper:    control B >= b
centered: abs(control B - mu) >= abs(b - mu)
```

Retain ties in both counts. Use exact Fractions for mu and centered comparisons;
never round the mean or replace the centered rule by doubling a one-sided tail.
For each count k among T=1000 new trials, report raw frequency k/T separately
from the plus-one score (k+1)/(T+1). Retain exact integer ratios and 50-digit
half-even decimal display strings; the score's minimum is 1/1001. Also retain
the exact new control mean and unbiased sample variance of B, the complete B
histogram and number of distinct control hashes as descriptive diagnostics.
No confidence interval, Gaussian approximation, multiplicity-adjusted decision,
language preference or reject/accept outcome is declared.

## Randomization assumptions and interpretation

The finite product of within-record position-permutation groups describes the
ideal bag null. Adding the identity/observed statistic and retaining inclusive
ties supplies conservative rank calibration with ideal independent uniform
group draws. [Hemerik and Goeman](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11749-017-0571-1)
state the group/random-draw conditions. The plus-one convention is not an exact
enumeration of this corpus's entire permutation tail.

The actual fixed-seed SHA stream is a deterministic pseudorandom implementation,
keyed in part by source bytes. This run neither proves ideal sampler uniformity/
independence nor supplies an exposure-valid discovery test. Scores are reported
as model-conditional randomization diagnostics; raw frequencies are simulation
estimates. Do not present them as the probability that a language/cipher model
is true or as exact manuscript-level significance. Related input variants and
the two reported tails are not independent confirmations.
