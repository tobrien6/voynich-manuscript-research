# Newbold's terminal photographs and Yale's labeled final surfaces

The photographic portions of Newbold/Kent 1928 Plates X and XV show broad
layout correspondence with Yale's ordinary image **1006277, labeled 116v**.
The faint upper text band, three longer bands beneath it, cross-shaped marks
and left-margin drawing arrangement provide a candidate surface association.
The fixed adjacent image **1006276, labeled 116r**, instead shows dense writing
and a vertical series of star-like margin marks. This selected comparison
does not identify the legacy catalog's `117v` or validate Newbold's readings.

supply no numeric folio designation. That key description is a historical

The adjacent 116r's dense text arrangement and star-like margin sequence differ
from both historical photographic portions. No corresponding sparse terminal
group or figure arrangement was observed there. This is one fixed adjacent
context comparison, not an all-folio search or a calibrated false-match test.
Crossing strokes within recto glyphs are not classified as the same marks
seen between the target's broad clusters. Fine differences in damage, tonal
reproduction or outline cannot establish fading, restoration, age or a change
to the manuscript between the source captures.

The photographic portions contain more visible upper text material than the
three-line hand/printed proposal on Plate XV separately presents. The fourth,
fainter upper band must remain a photographic observation. This is not a
claim about intentional omission, its script/language, or how any source
transcription should encode it. No transcript is copied into a corpus here.

## What the comparison supports

The selected overlap supports a **provisional historical-photograph to current
image association** for 116v. It does not authenticate the photograph's
capture history, prove exact stroke equivalence, identify every visible mark,
or verify the legacy `117v` alias. Numeric foliation is not visible in either
plate caption; the legacy catalog supplies no image identifier connecting its
label to this photograph. Qualified source/physical-surface review remains
necessary for a stronger association and for the separate catalog question.

The [S009 source ledger](newbold-terminal-source.json), all older source
records and the alias registry remain byte-identical to the accepted base.
The existing 11 alias tests and registry validation pass; actual legacy
lookup exits 1 with `mapping_allowed: false` and null target. Source/canvas
pin checks and all 20 planned plate/surface/feature records verify. These
checks preserve source identity and scope; they do not certify visual truth.
No new checker, tests, processed image, registration, confidence score,
statistical input, manuscript glyph transcription, decoding or translation
is introduced. The source PDF, full JPEGs and whole-page renders remain local.
