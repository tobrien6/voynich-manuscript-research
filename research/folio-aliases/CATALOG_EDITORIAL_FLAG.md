# An editorial marker beside the legacy terminal-folio label

The publisher-hosted [seventeen-page Spanish file](https://www.melusina.com/archivos/Voynich.pdf#page=9)
prints `117v [sic]` in its translated catalog passage: printed p. 24, PDF p. 9,
Part VI. The range remains 103r-117v. Printed pp. 22-23 (PDF pp. 7-8) separately
quote a description giving a final number 116, with reference callout 7.
That reference has not been checked. The visible title page supplies no
publication year adopted here.

This directly records an editorial mark following the catalog label. It does
not identify who inserted the mark, explain its reason, specify a replacement,
or connect the catalog entry to a particular canvas. The live
[Yale legacy record](https://pre1600ms.beinecke.library.yale.edu/docs/pre1600.ms408.HTM),
Part VI, still uses 117v without that immediately attached mark. Neither a bracketed marker nor a bare final number
establishes the proposed 117v-to-116v correspondence.

## Inspection and limits

Inspected 12 October 2026, America/New_York. The complete title page and PDF
pages 7-9 were rendered and visually inspected, including block typography,
the reference callout, range, bracket placement and printed footers. Poppler
reports 17 pages. A literal search of that file's text layer finds two `117v`
matches, one `[sic]`, and no `116v` match. This is a bounded search, not a claim
about the full book, original English edition, spelling variants or other
corrections.
