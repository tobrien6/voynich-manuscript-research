# Voynich section boundary inventory

Ten target folio sides from Beinecke MS 408 were inspected on 2026-10-11 using Yale's digitized images. The sample covers all six catalog sections under the [selection protocol](PROTOCOL.md). It records varied text arrangements and cases where a catalog section name is insufficient as a page-level illustration label. This boundary survey can complement inventories of section interiors; it establishes layout records, not readings of the script.

For the project's decipherment goal, these records help define comparable text regions before selecting and comparing transcriptions. Circular inscriptions, marginal signs, prose-like blocks, and shared foldout panels need distinct handling. All ten scans have been seen by this contributor; they must not be described as unseen held-out material for this contributor's later hypotheses.

## Sources and method

Section assignments follow the ranges in [Yale's manuscript catalog](https://pre1600ms.beinecke.library.yale.edu/docs/pre1600.ms408.HTM). The [Beinecke overview](https://beinecke.library.yale.edu/beinecke/collections/beinecke-cipher-voynich-manuscript) supplies the six broad subject categories. Folio labels and image identifiers come from [Yale's IIIF manifest](https://collections.library.yale.edu/manifests/2002046), linked by the [digital collection record](https://collections.library.yale.edu/catalog/2002046). See [source and access notes](SOURCES.md).

Each selected scan was visually inspected as a complete image, with its longest side rendered at 1600 pixels through the IIIF image service. Descriptions concern visible placement and forms at that resolution. No glyph, token or line counts were made. Descriptions for joint scans cover the canvas; they do not assign every panel or text region to the target side.

The machine-readable [selection metadata](selection.json) records original image dimensions, image service URLs, complete canvas labels and the manifest fingerprint. Zero-based indices below refer to that manifest version; canvas IDs are the more durable references.

## Visual observations and uncertainties

### Folio 1r

**Text layout:** Horizontal writing in several blocks separated by larger gaps, with some short endings toward the right. Enlarged or decorated initial forms appear near the left edges of blocks.

**Illustration forms:** Red flourishes and faint green patches are visible among and behind the writing. No clear, large plant specimen is visible on this side at the inspected resolution.

**Uncertainty:** Faint color could include show-through from the reverse; it is not classified as a botanical illustration. The catalog's botanical range is retained only as the sampling stratum. Faded areas need closer inspection for any transcription.

### Folio 66r

**Text layout:** Dense horizontal writing in several blocks occupies most of the upper page. A narrow vertical series of individual signs and short inscriptions sits to the left of the main writing. A large blank area separates the main text from small marks and writing at the bottom.

**Illustration forms:** A small reclining human-like outline and a few container-like or circular outlines appear near the bottom left. No large plant drawing is apparent on this side.

**Uncertainty:** The small forms are described by appearance, without identifying their function. The narrow marginal material should be annotated separately from the main text before counting tokens or lines.

### Folio 67r

**Text layout:** The scan contains two circular diagram panels side by side. Writing follows circular bands and radial divisions. There is a short horizontal block above the left diagram and horizontal writing below the right diagram, including red writing.

**Illustration forms:** The left circle has a central face, alternating colored rays and star-like marks. The right circle has a central radiating form and small round or crescent-like forms arranged around it.

**Uncertainty:** Yale labels the whole canvas `67r`; that label does not make its two panels a single uniform text region. Diagram orientation and panel boundaries need annotation before line-position comparisons. Astronomical meanings are not inferred from the visible forms.

### Folio 73r

**Text layout:** Writing runs around circular bands and appears as short inscriptions beside figures. A few figures and inscriptions extend above the main circle. There is little writing in a conventional rectangular paragraph layout.

**Illustration forms:** A central four-legged animal-like figure is surrounded by rings of human figures associated with star-like shapes.

**Uncertainty:** The animal and any zodiac attribution are not identified here. Whether an inscription labels a person, star or another feature requires a separately justified annotation rule.

### Folio 75r

**Text layout:** Horizontal writing fills the spaces to the left and right of a central vertical image, continues in a broad lower block, and narrows around a lower-right scene. Short inscriptions also occur within outlined shapes.

**Illustration forms:** Human figures appear within an elongated green area and a larger green basin-like area below it. A curved connector leads toward another group of figures at the lower right; the upper image includes blue-edged, funnel-like forms.

**Uncertainty:** Green areas are not evidence of a particular fluid or bodily process. Connections are visible lines; their depicted purpose remains unknown. Irregular text boundaries will matter when defining paragraph regions.

### Folio 84r

**Text layout:** A broad horizontal text block lies below the top scene, a second below the middle scene, and a narrower block to the right of a lower-left scene. Short inscriptions occur above or among the upper figures.

**Illustration forms:** Three groups of human figures occupy basin-like enclosures. The upper two have green interiors; the lower enclosure has blue marks. A winding blue-edged line descends along the left side, and a patterned canopy-like form extends over the top group.

**Uncertainty:** Bathing is the overview's section description, not a translation of the text. The lower scene and nearby writing should not automatically be treated as one entry.

### Folio 86r scanned with 85v

**Text layout:** Across the complete foldout, writing follows the borders and interiors of circular forms and some connecting paths. Small groups of horizontal writing also appear near the outer edges. Prominent folds cross the composition.

**Illustration forms:** Nine large circular medallions form an approximately three-by-three arrangement linked by patterned structures. They contain varied star-like marks, colored radial forms, small repeated shapes and architectural-looking outlines.

**Uncertainty:** This record describes the joint `85v and 86r (foldout)` canvas, not an isolated 86r panel. The visible features do not establish a map, cosmology or specific buildings. Text and image regions must be located independently of the physical fold lines.

### Folio 87r

**Text layout:** Two blocks of horizontal writing occupy the left and upper-central portion of the page and stop or narrow near the illustration.

**Illustration forms:** A large plant-like drawing dominates the right and lower page, with broad green leaves, branching stems, blue-tipped forms and a branching brown root. No row of jars is apparent on this side.

**Uncertainty:** No plant species or medicinal use is identified. Although the catalog places this folio in the pharmaceutical range, its visible layout differs from the rows of roots and vessels in the selected 102r canvas. Keep the catalog class and observed layout as separate fields.

### Folio 102r scanned with part of 101v

**Text layout:** The wide shared scan contains short inscriptions near plant-like drawings and multiple horizontal text blocks between or below illustrated rows. Several vertical cylindrical forms stand beside those rows. The left panel has grouped small plants above a lower text block.

**Illustration forms:** Green leaf shapes, branching brown or red root-like forms, and red or green cylindrical vessel-like forms occur across the scan. Multiple folded panels are visible.

**Uncertainty:** The manifest explicitly includes `101v (part)` as well as `102r`. These descriptions apply to the shared canvas. A panel-to-folio mapping has not been established, so the entire scan cannot be used as an isolated 102r text sample. Vessel contents and plant identities remain unassigned.

### Folio 103r

**Text layout:** Dense horizontal writing fills most of the page in visually separated groups. Red and unfilled star-like marks run down the left margin beside the writing. A brown discolored area overlies writing near the upper right.

**Illustration forms:** Marginal star-like marks provide the main visible ornament; no large plant or basin scene is apparent.

**Uncertainty:** A marginal star's alignment alone does not establish an entry's complete boundary. Discoloration and faint areas may affect a transcription. The text's subject is not inferred from the overview's tentative recipe interpretation.

## Coverage and implications

The ten target sides span all six catalog parts, but they do not constitute a representative sample of each illustration type. In particular, the two Part I boundary sides lack a clear large plant illustration at the inspected resolution, while Part V's 87r supplies a large plant layout. This is an observed limitation of the fixed sampling rule, not grounds for silently replacing selected folios.

The survey supports recording at least three independent properties in a future inventory: catalog section, observed illustration form, and text layout. It also supports treating a folio side, an image canvas and a text region as distinct units. Shared scans at 85v–86r and 101v–102r, and the two panels labeled 67r, require explicit region handling before any transcription statistics. Inventories using some of these same images provide additional descriptions of those images, not independent manuscript samples.

Boundary selection, unequal section sampling, omission of most versos, and qualitative inspection prevent estimates of manuscript-wide frequencies. Each image was reviewed by one AI-assisted observer. A second pass should add interior botanical sides, define panel boundaries for joint scans, and obtain independent review of the descriptions. Hypotheses about the writing should be tested on additional folios after choosing a documented transcription edition.

## Reproduce the source mapping

From the repository root, with Python 3 and network access:

```sh
python3 scripts/select_boundary_pilot.py --check research/boundary-pilot/selection.json
```

For a previously saved copy of the same manifest:

```sh
python3 scripts/select_boundary_pilot.py --manifest /path/to/2002046-manifest.json --check research/boundary-pilot/selection.json
```

The check compares all selected metadata and the exact downloaded manifest's SHA-256. A changed fingerprint requires review, even if the selected folios appear unchanged. To generate metadata for review, use `--output /path/to/new-selection.json`; do not overwrite the recorded selection without documenting changes. The script reproduces image references, not manual visual judgments. Image files are not included in this contribution.
