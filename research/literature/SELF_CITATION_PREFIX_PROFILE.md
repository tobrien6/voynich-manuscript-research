# Fixed generator prefix profile: six contrasts miss the seed ranges

The pinned self-citation setup fails the predefined descriptive profile criterion:
**24 of 32 related manuscript source/policy points** fall outside their matching
ranges across fixed seeds 1-31. The two last-slot y context profiles fall inside
in all four source/policy cases. All outcomes are retained in the
[complete result](self-citation-prefix-profile.json) and
[eight-panel figure](figures/self-citation-prefix-profile/profile.png).

This result concerns a chosen encoded-string bridge and this configuration/seed
list. It does not reject every copying mechanism or establish a language, cipher,
glyph reading or translation. A supplied-output replay is software evidence;
matching that file did not require matching these manuscript constraints.

## Interpretation and next test

The fixed profile does not meet this finite criterion on the declared encoded
cohort. The result does not establish why. Actual glyph identity, physical
line/locus equivalence and source/model paragraph correspondence remain unverified;
whole-locus and drawing exclusions may bias the represented manuscript. The
program has learned layout/frequency rules as well as copying, and its input
line is reused. Different group lengths and contextual mixtures also remain.

Strong mechanism conclusions require independently checked units and a separate
prospective comparison with controls/ablations, complete rule/settings identity
and an exposure-aware split. Any settings revised from these outcomes are
development work and need a new evaluation record. No translation follows from
a prefix contrast. Preserve the [comparison requirements](COPYING_MECHANISM.md)
and [decipherment protocol](../DECIPHERMENT_PROTOCOL.md).
