# Width-only wrapping changes an unconditional boundary-cost reference

The labelled IID toy process produces both signs of boundary edit-cost contrast
against its incoming-pair reference. Where eligible boundary mass is positive,
conditioning on the two boundary widths gives exact centered expectation zero under both registered finite weighting
schemes. A different reference distribution can therefore change an endpoint
similarity contrast without introducing dependence between incoming labels.

This follows the fixed [plan](PLAN.md), [settings](settings.json) and
[conditional-label proof](PROOF.md). It is known mathematical calibration on
synthetic strings, not a fitted account of manuscript similarities or a
replication of [the native endpoint method](../../transliteration/ENDPOINT_EDIT_DISTANCE.md)
or its [pending length-pattern control](https://github.com/tobrien6/voynich-manuscript-research/pull/179).
The separate [pending fixed-layout construction](https://github.com/tobrien6/voynich-manuscript-research/pull/188)
uses one-token loci with preset lengths and anchored identity permutations.
Here complete tokens are IID, row boundaries are generated by wrapping, and
the references are known incoming/within-width laws with variable edge counts.
These constructions address distinct mechanisms and reference distributions.

## All eight fixed outcomes

Lower normalized edit cost means greater similarity under the chosen metric.
Incoming-reference means are 5/9 for the uniform-three law, 35/64 for the
unequal-three law and 1/4 for the constant-width law. Every ordered label pair,
including equal labels, contributes with its actual probability. The unequal
width-two bin has label probabilities 3/4 and 1/4, giving mean cost 3/16 rather
than the uniform-bin value 1/4.

The table reports centered costs, not absolute costs or statistical tails.
Every exact uncentered mean remains in the [complete record](results.json).

Thus the four positive incoming-reference cases have less similarity under
that reference, while the two capacity-seven cases have greater similarity.
The constant-width zero is retained. This does not establish a direction at
other capacities, a manuscript magnitude match or the usefulness of a
particular empirical control. Individual width-centered residuals have both
signs; the expectation being zero does not make each stream zero.

The two estimands use different denominators:

| --- | ---: | ---: |

Pooled boundaries use E[sum cost]/E[N]. Equal eligible streams use
E[sum cost/N | N>0]. They are finite generator expectations, retain the initial
transient and need not coincide. Zero-boundary streams retain their probability,
trace and zero sums/counts, while their means stay undefined. At capacity 20
even a completely full final stream has no closing overflow and is excluded.

before the first CLI report calculation and first unit-test execution. The first

metric, geometry, censoring rule or weighting changed after outcomes. The
post-acceptance input-verification repair above updates only runtime provenance
and adds its two regression tests.
This is recorded chronology for known calibration, not independent exposure
certification or an untouched manuscript prediction.

The proof needs IID labels, deterministic additive widths and width-only
selection/weights. It relies on conditional label factorization at distinct
occurrence indices and linearity, with no row independence or variance claim.
Spelling-dependent selection, label dependence, contextual widths, paragraph
effects, drawings and manuscript writing mechanics are outside this model.
The capacities guarantee nonsingleton completed rows; a broader singleton
model could share one occurrence across two costs and would require explicit
shared-index accounting for variance.

To evaluate this mechanism against manuscript endpoints, first establish
reviewed physical-row/transcription mappings and freeze measured width,
spacing, vocabulary, boundary and missing-material rules before fitting or
evaluation. The known-law reference here is not the native anchored permutation
distribution. Previously exposed encoded strings, unresolved glyphs and source
dependencies cannot be turned into untouched evidence by this toy. No glyph
function, language, decipherment or translation follows.
