Equally likely orders can have nonzero scores

Under this fixed IID additive wrapping model, completed rows with the same endpoints and interior bag have equally likely interior orders. Their centered scores average to zero across the conditional orbit. An individual row or stream can still have a nonzero score.

What the model assumes

Positive integer widths are IID on a finite support with positive probabilities. Capacity is fixed and at least the largest supported width; the separator is fixed and nonnegative. Append on equality; close only on overflow; carry the same failed token into the next row. Conditioning must have positive probability and row selection must preserve the permitted permutations. The likelihood theorem requires at least two token positions; scoring requires at least five.

    @@LIMITS@@

Raw event weights condition on the first width. Order probabilities additionally condition on endpoints, the interior bag and completion. For an impossible condition the distribution and mean are undefined. A constant eligible interior uses the accepted zero convention.
